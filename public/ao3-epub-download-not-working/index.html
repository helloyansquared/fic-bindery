AO3 EPUB download not working? Here's why

AO3's EPUB, PDF, MOBI and AZW3 downloads are switched off for now. When AO3 came back on 15 November 2026 after a security incident, it said some features were temporarily disabled and that works can only be downloaded in HTML format. That was still the case on 26 November 2026. Your fic isn't broken and nothing is wrong with your account: download the HTML and convert it to EPUB, send the HTML to your Kindle, or wait for AO3 to bring the other formats back.

What happened to AO3 downloads

DateWhat changed
29 October 2026AO3 went offline after its team detected a security incident.
15 November 2026AO3 came back with some features temporarily disabled to reopen sooner: site icons, importing external works, and every download format except HTML.
26 November 2026Downloads are still HTML-only. AO3 hasn't given a date for EPUB, PDF, MOBI and AZW3.

Sources: AO3's notice at the top of its Downloading Fanworks FAQ and the AO3 news Tumblr. We update this page when AO3 does.

Apps and services that fetch works from AO3 for you are affected too: fic downloaders and e-reader plugins that ask AO3 for an EPUB get an error, and FicHub says AO3 works won't currently work.

Three ways to read the fic as an e-book now

1. Download the HTML and convert it to EPUB

  1. On the work, click Entire Work, then Download → HTML.
  2. Drop the file on FicToEpub and press Make EPUB. Chapters, tags, summary and notes are kept, and you get a cover and a table of contents. It runs in your browser, on phones too, and nothing is uploaded.
  3. Open the EPUB in Apple Books, Kobo, Google Play Books or KOReader, or send it to your Kindle.

On a computer, Calibre can convert the same HTML file if you already use it.

2. Send the HTML straight to a Kindle

Amazon's Send to Kindle accepts HTML files. The fic arrives readable but without a cover or chapter list, and it may be listed under a file name instead of the title. Converting to EPUB first gives a tidier book.

3. Wait for AO3's own downloads

AO3 describes the limit as temporary. When EPUB and AZW3 come back, they need no conversion. Check AO3's status posts rather than retrying the button.

Questions

Why can I only download AO3 works as HTML?

AO3 disabled the other download formats when it reopened on 15 November 2026 after a security incident, so it could come back sooner. As of 26 November 2026 only HTML downloads work, and AO3 hasn't announced when EPUB, PDF, MOBI and AZW3 return.

Is my AO3 account or the fic broken?

No. The limit applies to everyone. The HTML download contains the full work, with chapters, tags, summary and notes, so nothing is lost.

Why does my fic downloader or e-reader plugin say the EPUB link isn't found?

Those tools ask AO3 for its EPUB file, which AO3 isn't offering right now. Some can be switched to HTML; otherwise download the HTML from AO3 yourself and convert it.

When will AO3 EPUB downloads come back?

AO3 hasn't given a date. It called the disabled features temporary. This page is updated when AO3 announces a change.

Is converting the HTML allowed?

Downloading works for personal reading is an AO3 feature, and converting your own download to another format for your e-reader is the same thing AO3's EPUB button normally does. Keep the file for your own reading and leave kudos and comments on AO3.
