How to send AO3 fics to Kindle

To read an AO3 fic on a Kindle, download the work from AO3 as HTML (Entire Work → Download → HTML), convert it to EPUB with FicToEpub, then send the EPUB with Amazon's Send to Kindle: on the web at amazon.com/sendtokindle, from the Kindle app, or by email. It's free, and the book shows up in your Kindle library a few minutes later.

Convert your AO3 HTML to EPUB

Last updated 25 November 2026

Step 1: Turn the AO3 download into an EPUB

Normally AO3 offers EPUB and AZW3 downloads for e-readers. Since AO3 came back from its outage on 15 November 2026, readers report that only the HTML download works, so convert that file first:

  1. On the work, click Entire Work, then Download → HTML.
  2. Open FicToEpub and drop the HTML file in. The title, author, chapters, tags and summary are read from the file.
  3. Press Make EPUB and save the file. Nothing is uploaded: the EPUB is built in your browser.

Skip MOBI: Amazon no longer supports it for Send to Kindle. EPUB is the format to use.

Step 2: Send the EPUB to your Kindle

Amazon's Send to Kindle accepts EPUB files and converts them for your device. Pick whichever of the three ways is easiest:

On the web (easiest on a computer)

  1. Go to amazon.com/sendtokindle and sign in with the Amazon account your Kindle uses.
  2. Drag the EPUB in, or choose it from your files. Files can be up to 200 MB.
  3. Leave Add to your library on, pick your Kindle, and send.

From the Kindle app (easiest on a phone)

  1. Install the free Kindle app for iOS or Android and sign in.
  2. Find the saved EPUB in your Files or Downloads app and tap Share.
  3. Choose Kindle in the share sheet, then send.

By email

  1. Find your Send to Kindle address (it ends in @kindle.com) under Manage Your Content and Devices → Preferences → Personal Document Settings.
  2. On the same page, add the email you'll send from to the Approved Personal Document Email List. Mail from other addresses is ignored.
  3. Attach the EPUB and send it to your @kindle.com address. Amazon may email you once to confirm.

If the fic doesn't show up on your Kindle

  • Sent by email? Check that the address you sent from is on the Approved Personal Document Email List, and that you used the @kindle.com address of the right device.
  • Give it a few minutes, then sync your Kindle with Wi-Fi turned on.
  • Check your library filters. Personal documents can be listed separately from books you bought.
  • File too big for email? Use Send to Kindle for Web, which takes files up to 200 MB.
  • Amazon rejected the file? Make a fresh EPUB in FicToEpub and try again. Its output passes EPUBCheck in our tests.

AO3 to Kindle: questions

Can I send the AO3 HTML file straight to my Kindle?

You can, because Send to Kindle accepts HTML. But converting to EPUB first gives you a proper cover, the title and author in your Kindle library, and a chapter list you can jump around with.

Does Send to Kindle work with EPUB files?

Yes. Amazon has accepted EPUB through Send to Kindle since 2022 and converts it to Kindle format during delivery.

Does sending fics to Kindle cost anything?

No. FicToEpub is free, and Amazon's Send to Kindle is free for personal documents.

Can I put a whole series on my Kindle as one book?

Yes. Add every work's HTML file to FicToEpub in reading order, choose Merge into one book, then send the single EPUB to your Kindle.

Why can't I download the AO3 EPUB or AZW3 directly?

AO3 temporarily limited downloads to HTML while it restores features after its October–November 2026 outage. Once AO3 brings the other formats back you can download AZW3 or EPUB again; until then, convert the HTML.

Read on something other than a Kindle?

Kobo, Apple Books, Google Play Books, Boox and KOReader open the EPUB directly, with no Send to Kindle step. Make your EPUB here.
